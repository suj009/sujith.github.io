NRI onboarding: from a paper form to sign-up

Indians living abroad used to begin a FYERS account on a paper form. I designed the NRI path inside FYERS sign-up, on web and app, so an NRI starts where every other customer does, with their own international number.

The problem
NRIs began on a paper form, and an NRI application carries far more than a resident’s: an account type most people have never heard of, overseas address proof, tax-residency declarations and attested documents. One small mismatch sends it back.
My role
Design manager, and the designer on it: the NRI path inside FYERS sign-up, on web and app.
The call
Bring NRIs into the same sign-up as every other customer, settle the account type early, and collect the documents in the flow instead of on paper.
The result
Live on web and app. FYERS now describes NRI account opening as: sign up with your international number, choose the account type, submit the documents.
01

The situation

Opening an NRI account asks for much more than a resident account. On top of PAN and identity, an NRI needs a passport, overseas and Indian address proof, a linked NRE or NRO bank account, FEMA and FATCA declarations, and copies attested by an Indian embassy, a notary or a local banker. There are two account types, NRE PIS and NRO Non-PIS, each with its own banks, rules and segments.

FYERS’s own NRI posts say that applications are rarely held up by a missing document. It is the small mismatches: a name spelt differently on the PAN and the passport, an old overseas address, incomplete tax-residency details. And all of it used to begin on a paper form.

It is why FYERS held back on NRI accounts for so long. As its co-founder put it to a customer in the FYERS community in 2022: “The process is offline and is generally tedious due to the KYC regulations, logistics, time differences and so on.”

02

My role

As a design manager, I designed the NRI path inside FYERS sign-up myself, on web and in the app.

Who you worked with: the product manager, the developers, the NRI desk or compliance

03

The decisions

The call

Make NRI one of the account types at the very first step of sign-up, next to Demat and Corporate, right after the phone OTP, and let NRIs sign up with their own international number.

Why
An NRI is an investor first. A separate route makes them feel like an exception before they have begun, and loses people at the very first step. One flow also means one system to keep right.
The trade-off
A flow built for residents now has to branch, and every step only NRIs need adds length to it, so each one had to earn its place.
The call

Settle the account type early, as one of the first NRI steps.

Why
NRE PIS or NRO Non-PIS decides which bank can be linked, which documents are needed and what the customer can trade. Asking late means discovering a wrong choice after the documents are in.
The trade-off
A real decision, in unfamiliar terms, before the customer has seen any of the product.
The call

Collect the details and documents inside the flow, as uploads, instead of on paper.

Why
The details that most often hold an NRI application up, the name on the PAN and the passport, the overseas address, the tax residency, can be entered once and checked against each other before anything is sent.
The trade-off
The attested originals still have to travel, by courier, so the digital path cannot remove that step. It can only make sure what is sent is right the first time.
04

Outcome

The NRI path is live in FYERS sign-up on web and app. FYERS describes it publicly in three steps: use your international number, select the account type, and submit the required documents, with a dedicated NRI desk and DHL courier support for the originals that still have to travel. Customer posts in the FYERS community show NRI as one of the three account types at the first step of sign-up by September 2025.
